The reader of the menu's catalogs, snx_mo_lookup in src/gui/i18n.c, is musl's src/locale/__mo_lookup.c, rewritten
in C90 to read the catalog a byte at a time and to check that its tables are whole.
https://git.musl-libc.org/cgit/musl/tree/src/locale/__mo_lookup.c
